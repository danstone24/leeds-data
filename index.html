About Datamillnorth

Open data, made readable

How is Leeds actually doing?

Leeds City Council publishes hundreds of datasets — on potholes, spending, traffic, recycling and more — at Datamillnorth.org. Most of it is raw CSV. This site turns it into charts you can actually read.
