Source: City centre footfall on Datamillnorth, published by Leeds City Council. Last updated —.

Eight cameras count people walking past fixed points in the city centre, hour by hour. The published files overlap heavily and have changed format several times since 2008, and the cameras have been renamed and occasionally relocated; we merge everything and de-duplicate each hour, keeping the council's later, revised (higher) figures where they conflict. Because the number of working cameras varies, we show the average count per camera per day rather than raw totals. Treat it as a good indicator of the trend, not a turnstile-exact count.
